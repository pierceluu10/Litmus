Knobs you may change (anything else is impossible) with their BASELINE values:
$knob_schema

YOUR STAR: $star

THE OTHER AGENTS OF YOUR GENERATION (read their results and suggestions; build on them, never repeat them): $siblings

THE HUMAN'S LIBRARY (annotations are the human's own doubts): $claims
THE HUMAN'S ANSWERS AND FEEDBACK ABOUT YOUR STAR (each MUST be implemented by one of your options): $agenda
INSTRUCTION: $instruction

Return:
- take: one or two plain sentences, in the first person, on what your result means and where you would go next.
  Mention another agent's result when it changes your plan (e.g. "Since the Flip agent confirmed alternating flip helps, ...").
- proposals: 1 to $most options for the next experiment, the most informative first. Each changes ONE idea relative to

  parent and change BOTH your method's knob and epochs (e.g. flip="random" and epochs=20): Litmus re-runs the parent
  at the same epochs, so the comparison isolates your method. Changing only epochs on your own star tests longer
  training, not your method. A "combine" option may stack your change with
  another agent's confirmed change. Ground each in claim_ids from the library and/or agenda_ids. predicted = the
  direction the claim predicts relative to the parent. condition_notes: whether our testbed matches the paper's
  conditions. Set only changed knobs in knob_changes. Never re-run an existing configuration.
